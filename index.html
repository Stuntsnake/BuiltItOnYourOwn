About Us

Welcome to North Scott's Weather Club. We are a group of students with a passion for forcasting, chasing, and watching our local skies. All students who share our passion are welcome to join!

Educational Opprotunities

We work with the National Weather Service to provide unique educational opprotunities for our members. In the spring, students have the opprotunity to earn their Skywarn© storm spotting certification.

Community Service

We are dedicated to giving back in our community whenever possible. During severe weather events, members of the club may attend special meeting where we relay real time information to the public. Members are also encoraged to group together and chase storms on their own, bringing critical ground truth to the NWS's forcasters. After storms, we also organize first aid efforts and run fundraisers to help affected communities recover.
