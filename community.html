Storm Chasing & Weather Club

North Scott High School

Community Service

We are dedicated to giving back in our community whenever possible. During severe weather events, members of the club may attend special meeting where we relay real time information to the public. Members are also encoraged to group together and chase storms on their own, bringing critical ground truth to the NWS's forcasters. After storms, we also organize first aid efforts and run fundraisers to help affected communities recover.
